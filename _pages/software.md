## Open-source software development

<ol reversed class="small-list">
	<li> <strong>2022, Feb. - present</strong>. Core developer of <strong>PyGeoN</strong>, a Python package for Geo-Numerics. PyGeoN is developed by the Geosciences &amp; Protection of Land and Water Resources group at MOX laboratory, <a href="https://www.polimi.it/en">Politecnico di Milano</a>, Italy.<br>Repository: <a href="https://github.com/compgeo-mox/pygeon">https://github.com/compgeo-mox/pygeon</a> </li>
	<li> <strong>2017, May - 2022, Feb.</strong> Core developer of <strong>PorePy</strong>, a simulation tool for fractured and deformable porous media written in Python. PorePy is developed by the Porous Media Group at the University of Bergen, Norway.<br>Repository: <a href="https://github.com/pmgbergen/porepy">https://github.com/pmgbergen/porepy</a> </li>
	<li> <strong>2010, Jan. - 2013, Feb.</strong> Member of the administrative board and developer community of the parallel finite element library <strong>LifeV</strong>. LifeV is developed by the groups CMCS (EPFL, Switzerland), E(CM)2 (Emory University, USA), MOX (<a href="https://www.polimi.it/en">Politecnico di Milano</a>, Italy), and REO/ESTIME (INRIA, France).<br>Website: <a href="http://www.lifev.org">http://www.lifev.org</a><br>Repository: <a href="https://github.com/lifev/lifev">https://github.com/lifev/lifev</a> </li>
</ol>
