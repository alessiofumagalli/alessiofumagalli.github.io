## Research outline

Starting from my Ph.D., my research focuses on advanced mathematical models and non-standard numerical schemes for flow in fractured porous media, with emphasis on:

1. Hybrid-dimensional/reduced models for transport and flow in complex fracture systems.
2. Numerical methods (including XFEM and VEM) to relax geometric constraints and improve computational efficiency.
