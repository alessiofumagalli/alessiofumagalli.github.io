Starting from my Ph.D., the central thread of my research has been to introduce, extend, and analyze advanced mathematical models and non-standard numerical schemes to improve the understanding of flow in fractured porous media.

Main directions:

1. Reduced order and hybrid-dimensional models for scalar transport and single-phase or multi-phase flow in complex fracture systems.
2. Non-standard numerical methods, including XFEM and VEM, to relax geometric constraints and make simulations on complex domains more affordable.
